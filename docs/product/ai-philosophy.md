# AI Philosophy

> **AI prepares, checks, calculates, and flags. Humans authorize.**

## AI Role
- **Review**: AI analyzes Check Requests for completeness.
- **Detection**: Flags anomalies or missing evidence.
- **Assistance**: Helps speed up the QA process.

## Human Role
- **Decision**: All approvals (Accept/Reject) require human action.
- **Audit**: Every human action is recorded in the Audit trail.
